# Mercy Writing Bureau

Build a premium, modern, and conversion-focused portfolio website for my professional ghostwriting service.

I will upload my profile picture. Use the actual picture on the website and analyze its dominant colors, lighting, background, clothing, and overall mood. Build the website’s color palette and visual identity around the photograph so everything feels cohesive and intentionally branded.

Do not alter my facial features or replace my picture with an AI-generated person. If the photograph has a busy background, present it inside an elegant frame, cutout, gradient, or softly blended container.

BRAND DIRECTION

The website should feel:

• Sophisticated
• Warm and personal
• Editorial and literary
• Trustworthy
• Premium but approachable
• Creative without looking childish
• Suitable for serious authors, entrepreneurs, coaches, and publishers

Avoid generic AI website styling, excessive gradients, cluttered layouts, stock photos of random people, gold-heavy designs, and unnecessary animations.

Use elegant typography inspired by professionally published books. Pair a distinctive serif font for headings with a clean, readable sans-serif font for body text.

WEBSITE STRUCTURE

Create the following pages and sections:

HOME PAGE

Navigation:

• Home
• About
• Services
• Portfolio
• Process
• Testimonials
• FAQ
• Contact

Hero section:

Headline: Your Story. Professionally Written in Your Voice.

Supporting text: I help authors, entrepreneurs, professionals, and storytellers transform their ideas into compelling, publish-ready books without losing the voice and vision that make their stories unique.

Primary button: Discuss Your Book

Secondary button: View My Portfolio

Place my uploaded profile picture prominently within the hero section. Add subtle book, manuscript, or editorial design elements without making the layout busy.

Add a small trust bar beneath the hero:

• Original, Confidential Writing
• Full Copyright Transfer
• Research-Based Content
• Publishing-Ready Manuscripts

ABOUT SECTION

Heading: Your Ideas Deserve the Right Words

Write a warm and professional introduction explaining that I help clients develop ideas, structure their books, capture their voices, and produce polished manuscripts.

Mention my experience in fiction and nonfiction ghostwriting, including business books, memoirs, personal development, leadership, mystery, thriller, romance, and other selected genres.

Do not invent my years of experience, number of books completed, qualifications, awards, bestseller claims, or sales figures. Use editable placeholders whenever verified information has not been provided.

Include a button: More About Me

SERVICES SECTION

Create attractive service cards for:

• Nonfiction Ghostwriting
• Business and Leadership Books
• Memoir and Autobiography Writing
• Fiction Ghostwriting
• Mystery and Thriller Writing
• Book Outlines and Story Development
• Developmental Editing
• Book Editing and Proofreading
• KDP Formatting and Publishing Support

Each card should include a concise, benefit-focused description and a “Learn More” link.

FEATURED PORTFOLIO SECTION

Create a visually impressive portfolio containing 20 separate projects from my past client work.

Use the actual project information and cover images I upload. Never fabricate a client, book title, testimonial, result, publishing status, or book cover.

Display the projects in a responsive filterable grid. Include category filters such as:

• All
• Business
• Memoir
• Personal Development

• Other Nonfiction

Each project card should contain:

• Book cover or approved project image
• Project title
• Genre or category
• Service delivered
• Word count, if provided
• Short project summary
• “View Case Study” button

When a visitor clicks “View Case Study,” open a dedicated project page or polished modal containing:

• Project overview
• Client’s objective
• Genre
• Scope of work
• My contribution

• Final deliverables

For confidential projects, allow the title and client identity to be anonymized while still presenting the genre, challenge, process, and completed deliverables.

WORKING PROCESS

Create a clear six-step process:

Discovery and Project Brief

Research and Book Strategy

Outline Development

Sample Chapter Approval

Manuscript Writing and Revisions

Final Editing and Delivery

Present the steps in a polished timeline or numbered card layout.

WHY WORK WITH ME

Include the following benefits:

• Writing tailored to the client’s natural voice
• Original content developed for each project
• Confidentiality and NDA availability
• Clear milestone-based communication
• Research-driven storytelling
• Collaborative revisions
• Full ownership transferred after final payment
• Publishing-ready final files

Do not include guarantees that cannot be verified.

TESTIMONIALS

Create a tasteful testimonial section using only testimonials I provide.

Each testimonial should include:

• Client name or anonymous label
• Project type
• Testimonial
• Optional client image or initials

If testimonials have not yet been uploaded, use obvious placeholders such as “Add verified client testimonial here.” Do not write fake reviews.

FAQ SECTION

Include these questions:

• What types of books do you ghostwrite?
• Will I own the copyright to my book?
• Is my project kept confidential?
• Can you sign an NDA?
• How does the ghostwriting process work?
• Can you help if I only have an idea?
• Do you provide editing and formatting?
• How long does it take to complete a book?
• How are payments and milestones structured?
• How many revisions are included?

Write reassuring but accurate answers. Use editable placeholders for policies, pricing, turnaround times, and revision limits that I have not supplied.

CONTACT SECTION

Heading: Let’s Turn Your Idea Into a Book

Supporting text: Tell me about your book idea, current progress, preferred word count, and publishing goals. I’ll review the details and recommend the best next step.

Create a professional inquiry form with:

• Full name
• Email address
• Book genre
• Service needed
• Estimated word count
• Current project stage
• Preferred completion date
• Estimated budget range
• Project description
• File upload
• Confidentiality or NDA requirement
• Consent checkbox

Add a strong submission button: Submit Your Book Project

After submission, show a polished confirmation message.

FOOTER

Include:

• Short professional introduction
• Navigation links
• Services links
• Email address placeholder
• Social media placeholders
• Copyright notice
• Privacy Policy
• Terms and Conditions
• “Available for Select Projects” indicator

FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

• Build the website for desktop, tablet, and mobile
• Use smooth but subtle scrolling and hover effects
• Keep text readable with strong color contrast
• Optimize uploaded images without damaging their quality
• Add descriptive alt text to images
• Use reusable components for portfolio entries
• Make all content easy to edit later
• Add a sticky navigation menu
• Add clear calls to action throughout the website
• Add portfolio filtering and project detail pages
• Add form validation and clear error messages
• Include basic SEO titles and descriptions
• Use semantic headings and accessible form labels
• Ensure fast loading performance
• Do not use lorem ipsum in the finished design
• Do not invent professional credentials or client results

SEO DIRECTION

Naturally optimize the website for relevant phrases such as:

• Professional ghostwriter
• Book ghostwriting services
• Fiction ghostwriter
• Nonfiction ghostwriter
• Business book ghostwriter
• Memoir ghostwriter
• Book writer for hire
• Ghostwriting and book editing services

Keep the writing natural and client-focused. Do not stuff keywords unnaturally.

FINAL DESIGN GOAL

The finished website should immediately communicate that I am a skilled, dependable, and professional ghostwriter who can turn a client’s ideas into a well-structured, engaging, publish-ready manuscript.

The design must be visually connected to my uploaded profile picture and should look custom-built for my personal brand. It should not resemble a generic template or an AI-generated website.

Before finalizing the site, make sure all 20 portfolio projects are included, every page works correctly, the mobile version looks polished, and every button leads to the correct section or page.
